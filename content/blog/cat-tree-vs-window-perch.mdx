If you are choosing between a cat tree and a window perch, the right answer usually depends on floor space, your cat’s energy, and how much visual presence you can tolerate in the room.

## Choose a cat tree if

- Your cat likes climbing and height
- You need scratching and lounging in a single piece
- You have room for a dedicated vertical zone

## Choose a window perch if

- Your space is limited
- Your cat is more of a watcher than a climber
- You want a lighter visual solution

## The design question

A cat tree makes a bigger statement. That can be worth it if the structure is stable, material-led, and thoughtfully proportioned. A perch is easier to integrate but offers less enrichment overall.
