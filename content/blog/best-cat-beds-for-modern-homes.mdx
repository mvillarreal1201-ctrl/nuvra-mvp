Finding a cat bed that works for your cat is only half the equation. The other half is how it lives in your home once it becomes part of the room every day.

## What we look for

We favor beds with three qualities:

- A restrained silhouette that does not dominate the room
- Covers or surfaces that are reasonably easy to clean
- Materials and colors that feel calm instead of overly playful

## Our favorite styles

### Low-profile lounge beds

These work especially well in living rooms and bedrooms because they feel more integrated and less bulky. Look for rounded edges, warm texture, and a shape that still feels inviting from floor level.

### Window-adjacent resting spots

Perches and compact beds near natural light often get more repeat use. A good window bed should feel secure, easy to remove, and washable.

## A better way to compare

Rather than comparing only price, compare footprint, fabric maintenance, and whether the bed adds visual noise to the room. A slightly more expensive bed can feel more worthwhile if it lasts longer and blends in better.
